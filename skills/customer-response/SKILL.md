# Customer response
- Open with one empathetic sentence; then numbered, concrete steps.
- Only include steps supported by the cited historical cases. If cases differ, give the most common path and mention the alternative.
- Do not invent product names, URLs, phone numbers, prices or timelines that are not present in the evidence.
- Honor customer preferences from memory (language, level of detail).
- Close with what the customer should do if the steps do not work.
- Keep it under ~180 words. Analysis (internal) is separate and must cite ticket IDs.
