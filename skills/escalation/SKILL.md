# Escalation
Escalate when any applies:
- No relevant historical case, or retrieved cases conflict and clarification did not resolve it.
- Priority is critical, or there is security, data-loss, legal, or payment-dispute risk.
- The fix needs account-specific action or access that a reply cannot provide.
Escalation output: reason (one sentence, evidence-based), target queue (from similar cases or triage), and a brief note of what was already tried. A holding message to the customer must not promise outcomes or timelines not seen in evidence.
